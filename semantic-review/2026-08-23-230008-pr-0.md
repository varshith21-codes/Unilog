## The proof specimen mixes identifiers and policy values from different artifacts

This is a **confirmed, blocking** truthfulness issue in `apps/console/src/app/page.tsx:76-95`. The module names `BA-100-025` and reports “Decision score 0.728 / policy gate 0.707.” The corresponding persisted artifact is `BA-100-075`; its `body_material` decision is `0.7283` against `0.685`, with the same class, material, source quote, and page locator. No repository artifact ties `BA-100-025` to the displayed score or a `0.707` publication gate.

The module is not labeled as an illustrative composite, conflicting with the requirement for truthful copy and the presentation’s instruction to label fixture-backed data accurately. Use the persisted `BA-100-075` specimen and threshold, or remove the record-specific numbers and identify the surface as illustrative.

## The verified-span badge is laid over the preceding source row

This is a **likely, non-blocking** responsive craft defect in `apps/console/src/app/globals.css:2108-2130`. The highlighted row follows “Series / BA-100,” but its `::before` badge starts at the highlight’s top edge and translates upward by 115% without reserved block space. “Verified span” therefore shares the preceding row’s vertical band with its right-aligned `BA-100` value, with less room when text wraps.

Reserve space above the highlight, move the badge inside it, or attach it to a dedicated header area. Verify the result at 360px and in both themes.

</details>
